Cute Bunny Lumine

It's a bunny suit, but it has that blend between cuteness and skimpiness that might please several people, so enjoy ^^

WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT THIS MOD

It has a toggle (P) between:
- Jacket version (when the jacket is present, the tail is tied to it, because if it was in the body it would clip with the jacket a lot)
- No jacket (with the bunny tail in the body)
- No jacket, no bunny tail (because I know what some people want)

If the mod breaks with future updates, don't forget to check under genshin tools on gamebanana for scripts to fix all mods at once. 
Look for the most recent version of this tool (likely will be a new post): https://gamebanana.com/tools/15654 
There is no point in modders updating all of their individual posts when those fixes exist, so please don't ask. That goes for my older mods too.

And (I paste this in all post just to spread the info, it's not necessarily relevant to this mod) don't forget that, 
for characters released after Sumeru, mods require you to have the ORFix.ini file in your BufferValues folder (inside the Mods folder).
That's what fixes the green reflection in the character menu, and outlines in the wrong color when it's not a lightmap issue.
You can get it here: https://github.com/leotorrez/LeoTools/blob/main/releases/ORFix.ini
